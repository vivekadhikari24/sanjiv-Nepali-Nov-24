Master Sanjiv Nepali is a globally recognized champion who has earned over 35 international gold medals. He competes primarily in Poomsae.

What is Poomsae? In Taekwondo, Poomsae (pronounced poom-say) is a set of artistic, non-combat patterns of movement. Players are judged on their strength, balance, focus, and perfect control.

Master Sanjiv has successfully hoisted the national flag of Nepal on the world stage, winning matches in the USA, United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, Spain, Thailand, Hong Kong, Macau, Indonesia, and the Philippines.
