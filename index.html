Meet Master Sanjiv

From Nijgadh to the World Stage

Hailing from the small town of Nijgadh, Nepal, Master Sanjiv Nepali's journey to the United States is a powerful narrative of resilience, passion, and relentless pursuit of martial arts perfection. Overcoming geographical and resource barriers, he has established himself as a prominent figure on the global Taekwondo stage.

“Taekwondo is about building a strong mind, cultivating respect, and ultimately, helping others find their strength.”

— Master Sanjiv Nepali
